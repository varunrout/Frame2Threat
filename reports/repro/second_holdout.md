# Second sealed holdout (UEFA Euro 2020) and a position-only boosted baseline

Scored **once** under [second_holdout_protocol.md](second_holdout_protocol.md), committed before any of it
was scored. The committed models, thresholds and features are unchanged. Every number below is read from
[second_holdout.json](second_holdout.json); this page is generated by `scripts/render_second_holdout.py`.

Euro 2020: 51 matches, 8,156 possessions, 149,594 eligible snapshots (danger prevalence 0.382). No pipeline fix was needed and the run was not repeated.

The new baseline (`GBM_pos`) was **added after the Euro 2024 scoring** and frozen before it was scored on any target. It uses only cur_x, cur_y, dist_goal, angle_goal, zone, attack_dir (no event history; `attack_dir` is constant in the possessing-team frame), was fitted on the original 69 training matches and selected on the 15 validation matches only: {'max_depth': 3, 'min_child_weight': 1, 'n_estimators': 170, 'val_logloss': 0.6083191343273535}.

## What this shows and what it does not

- In every target the lift of the event-time models over the nonlinear position-only baseline is smaller than their lift over the linear ball x/y baseline (compare the difference rows). Part of the earlier lift came from the linear baseline's inability to model the goal's geometry, not from the event history.
- On the 15 in-cohort test matches that smaller lift is not distinguishable from zero on all snapshots; it is on the two larger external holdouts and on one snapshot per possession.
- On Euro 2020 the GRU ranks slightly below XGBoost (difference row above); on Euro 2024 and the test split they are indistinguishable.
- Calibration: on Euro 2020 both baselines have a lower ECE than both models (table above). The scores are a ranking aid, not calibrated probabilities.
- Two men's international tournaments are two external tests. Training used 64 World Cup 2022 and 35 Barcelona La Liga 2020/21 matches, so these are close in kind to part of the training data. Club leagues, other seasons and women's football are untested.
- The models' earlier results reproduce exactly on the test split and Euro 2024: True (`reproduces_earlier_model_results` in the JSON). Euro 2024 numbers for the models are unchanged; only the new baseline and its differences are new there.
