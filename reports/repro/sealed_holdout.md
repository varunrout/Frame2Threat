# Sealed holdout: UEFA Euro 2024

One external test of the committed event-time models, scored **once** under the protocol fixed
before any holdout input was built: [sealed_holdout_protocol.md](sealed_holdout_protocol.md). No model,
threshold, feature or hyper-parameter was changed. Numbers: [sealed_holdout.json](sealed_holdout.json)
(holdout) and [frozen_metrics_test.json](frozen_metrics_test.json) (original test split, same code and
definitions). This page is generated by `scripts/render_sealed_holdout.py`.

Holdout: UEFA Euro 2024 (men), 51 matches, 7,066 possessions, 145,649 eligible snapshots (danger prevalence 0.426). In-cohort test: 15 matches, 2,284 possessions, 43,497 snapshots (prevalence 0.405). No pipeline fix was needed; the holdout was scored in a single run.

## Verdict

- **Event-time XGBoost:** holdout ROC AUC 0.662 [0.650, 0.674]; the reference 0.665 lies inside the interval, so by the pre-specified rule AUC 0.66 **generalised** to Euro 2024. Lift over ball x/y: 0.021 [0.015, 0.027], which holds.
- **Event-time GRU:** holdout ROC AUC 0.662 [0.650, 0.674]; the reference 0.665 lies inside the interval, so by the pre-specified rule AUC 0.66 **generalised** to Euro 2024. Lift over ball x/y: 0.021 [0.013, 0.028], which holds.

## What this shows and what it does not

- It shows that the committed models rank Euro 2024 snapshots about as well as they ranked the 15 in-cohort test matches, on 51 matches never used for training, validation or threshold choice, and that the small lift over ball position is still there.
- It does not make the score a good early-warning tool: the AUC is still modest, most of the signal is still ball position, and an alert still fires on a large share of safe possessions (table above).
- Calibration is worse on the holdout than in-cohort (ECE above). Treat the score as a ranking, not a probability.
- It is one competition: international tournament football, men, 360-era data. It says nothing yet about other leagues, seasons, club football outside the training cohort, or women's football. The training cohort already contained a World Cup, so this is the closest kind of competition, not the hardest test.
- Snapshots within a possession are correlated; the intervals resample whole matches.
- The in-cohort column re-scores the committed models with today's arrays builder: 43,497 test snapshots and AUC 0.6639 (XGBoost) / 0.6623 (GRU). The documented re-score (0.6649 / 0.6636, [artefact_check.md](artefact_check.md); 43,541 snapshots in [repair_evaluation.json](repair_evaluation.json)) was computed on an earlier build of the same 99 matches. The cause of the difference has not been established (see CLAIMS.md open item 4 for a related known difference). The holdout uses today's builder, so the in-cohort column is the like-for-like comparison.
